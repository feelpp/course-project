== Time and format

* *Date:* Monday 5 October 2026.
* *Time:* 10:00–12:00, Paris time (Europe/Paris).
* *Proposed format:* 15 minutes per topic, comprising 10 minutes of presentation and 5 minutes for questions and transition.
* *Speakers:* proposers of the M2 S3 2026–2027 project topics.

Topics by the same speaker are grouped together.
Remote presentations are scheduled first, followed by in-person presentations.

====
This is a proposed schedule based on the availability declared in the spreadsheet as of 2 October 2026.
*Alessandro De Giorgi's* participation, marked “Maybe”, remains *to be confirmed*.
