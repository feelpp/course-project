== Research institutions

=== CEMOSIS

https://www.cemosis.fr/[CEMOSIS — Centre for Modelling and Simulation in Strasbourg] is a mathematics platform within IRMA, supported by the University of Strasbourg and CNRS.
It connects academic research with industry and other disciplines through mathematical modelling, simulation and optimisation, high-performance computing, data science, and signal and image processing.

The xref:2026-2027/m2-s3/topics/ktirio-facades-3d.adoc[Ktirio 3D façade analysis project] proposed by Christophe Prud'homme combines building geometry, image analysis and energy simulation in collaboration with NEPSEN.

=== IAC-CNR

https://www.iac.cnr.it/[IAC — Istituto per le Applicazioni del Calcolo “Mauro Picone”] is an applied-mathematics research institute of Italy's National Research Council (CNR), headquartered in Rome.
It develops mathematical, statistical and computational models and methods for interdisciplinary problems in science, society and industry, including medical imaging and biology.

The xref:2026-2027/m2-s3/topics/ooc-kde-3d.adoc[3D kernel density estimation project] proposed by Elishan Christian Braun uses cell-tracking data to support the calibration and validation of cancer-on-chip digital twins.

=== CNR Nanotec

https://nanotec.cnr.it/home/[CNR Nanotec — Institute of Nanotechnology] is a research institute of Italy's National Research Council (CNR).
It conducts fundamental and applied research in nanoscience and nanotechnology, combining physics, chemistry, engineering, materials science, biology and medicine with experimental techniques and modelling tools.

The xref:2026-2027/m2-s3/topics/ooc-migration-tumeur-astrocytes.adoc[tumour–astrocyte migration project] proposed by Alessandro De Giorgi connects registered organ-on-chip imaging and cell trajectories to calibrated PDE models with quantified uncertainty.

=== LNCMI

https://lncmi.cnrs.fr/en/[LNCMI — National High Magnetic Field Laboratory] is a CNRS laboratory providing high-magnetic-field facilities for research, with sites in Grenoble and Toulouse.
Grenoble operates continuous-field magnets, while Toulouse specialises in pulsed fields; the laboratory combines scientific experiments with magnet and instrumentation development.

The xref:2026-2027/m2-s3/topics/lncmi-aimants-resistifs.adoc[resistive-magnet modelling project] proposed by Christophe Trophime develops a simplified circuit model coupled to the cooling system, with parameter identification and possible data assimilation.

== Company partners

=== Avnir Energy

https://www.avnir-energy.fr/[Avnir Energy] is a company developing vibration-monitoring and structural-health-monitoring solutions for the energy sector.
Its custom monitoring systems combine physical measurements with numerical models, including applications in vibration, acoustics and mechanical behaviour.

The two CSMI projects proposed by Gabriel Georges address xref:2026-2027/m2-s3/topics/avnir-imagerie-topologique.adoc[topological imaging for defect detection] and the xref:2026-2027/m2-s3/topics/avnir-gravite-ondes.adoc[effect of gravity on wave propagation in slender structures], in the context of monitoring nuclear-industry structures.

=== Willing AI / Coruscant

https://coruscant.fr/en/[Coruscant — Willing AI] combines expertise in applied mathematics and artificial intelligence with the digital-transformation experience of https://willing.fr/[Willing].
It develops AI solutions for organisations, with an emphasis on security, reliability and responsible use of data.

The xref:2026-2027/m2-s3/topics/willing-arbovirus-control.adoc[arbovirus epidemic-control project] supervised by Mario Veruete explores reaction–diffusion modelling and mosquito-release strategies.
A follow-on internship, permanent employment (CDI) and/or a PhD may be possible.

=== NEPSEN

https://nepsen.fr/[NEPSEN] is an independent French engineering and consulting company specialising in the energy and ecological transition.
It supports public and private clients in sustainable buildings, renewable energy and industrial energy efficiency, with services including energy audits, technical studies and project engineering.

For the xref:2026-2027/m2-s3/topics/ktirio-facades-3d.adoc[Ktirio 3D façade analysis project], NEPSEN proposes three reference sites and supporting design and monitoring documents for validation, subject to data availability.
