1. Time and format

  • Date: Monday 5 October 2026.

  • Time: 10:00–12:00, Paris time (Europe/Paris).

  • Videoconference: Join the presentation on Zoom.

  • Proposed format: 12 minutes per topic, comprising 9 minutes of presentation and 3 minutes for questions and transition.

3. Company partners

3.1. Avnir Energy

Avnir Energy is a company developing vibration-monitoring and structural-health-monitoring solutions for the energy sector. Its custom monitoring systems combine physical measurements with numerical models, including applications in vibration, acoustics and mechanical behaviour.

The two CSMI projects proposed by Gabriel Georges address topological imaging for defect detection and the effect of gravity on wave propagation in slender structures, in the context of monitoring nuclear-industry structures.

3.2. Willing AI / Coruscant

Coruscant — Willing AI combines expertise in applied mathematics and artificial intelligence with the digital-transformation experience of Willing. It develops AI solutions for organisations, with an emphasis on security, reliability and responsible use of data.

The arbovirus epidemic-control project supervised by Mario Veruete explores reaction–diffusion modelling and mosquito-release strategies. A follow-on internship, permanent employment (CDI) and/or a PhD may be possible.

3.3. NEPSEN

NEPSEN is an independent French engineering and consulting company specialising in the energy and ecological transition. It supports public and private clients in sustainable buildings, renewable energy and industrial energy efficiency, with services including energy audits, technical studies and project engineering.

For the Ktirio 3D façade analysis project, NEPSEN proposes three reference sites and supporting design and monitoring documents for validation, subject to data availability.
