CSMI Projects — M2 S3 2026–2027

This space brings together the CSMI Master 2, Semester 3 (S3) project proposals for the 2026–2027 academic year.

1. Proposed topics

Ten topics are available from Avnir Energy, CNR Nanotec, IAC-CNR, IRMA, LNCMI, CEMOSIS in collaboration with NEPSEN, and Willing AI / Coruscant.

  • Topic catalogue and detailed project pages.

  • Proposal spreadsheet.

2. Topic presentations — Monday 5 October 2026

The session runs from 10:00 to 12:00, Paris time, with in-person and remote presentations according to the preferences declared by the project proposers.

  • Proposed presentation order and time slots.

3. Organisation

Each project page specifies the proposed team size and possible follow-on internships or PhD opportunities. Project proposers present their topics before students select their projects.
